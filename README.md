# 🌲 Forest Dash: Spirit Runner

**Forest Dash: Spirit Runner** is an exciting 2D endless runner game set in a magical autumn woodland filled with ancient secrets, glowing runestones, mysterious spirits, and hidden treasures. Embark on an endless adventure where quick reflexes, perfect timing, and smart decisions determine how far you can run.

Take control of a fearless spirit runner and explore an enchanted forest filled with challenging obstacles and magical surprises. Jump over ancient glowing runestones, slide beneath flying spirit birds, and navigate through increasingly challenging environments. Every second brings a new challenge, making each run a unique and thrilling experience.

## ✨ Features

- **Dynamic Platforming:** Experience smooth and responsive movement with jumping, double jumping, and sliding mechanics.
- **Spirit Shield:** Activate a magical shield to protect yourself from dangerous obstacles and survive challenging situations.
- **Coin Magnet:** Attract nearby golden star coins and collect rewards effortlessly.
- **Radiant Star Coins:** Gather glowing coins throughout your journey to increase your score and unlock exciting rewards.
- **Score Multipliers:** Build higher scores using multipliers and demonstrate your running skills.
- **Unlockable Runner Skins:** Visit the in-game Wardrobe to discover and unlock different character appearances.
- **Enchanting Environment:** Explore a beautifully designed autumn woodland featuring magical lighting, glowing objects, and atmospheric surroundings.
- **Increasing Difficulty:** Test your reflexes as obstacles become more challenging the longer you survive.
- **Cross-Platform Controls:** Enjoy smooth and responsive controls optimized for both desktop and mobile devices.

## 🎮 Gameplay

The objective is simple: run as far as possible, avoid obstacles, collect golden coins, and achieve the highest score. Use precise jumps and sliding maneuvers to overcome challenges while activating power-ups strategically to extend your survival.

## 🏆 Your Adventure Begins

Whether you are chasing a new high score, collecting magical rewards, or unlocking unique runner skins, **Forest Dash: Spirit Runner** offers an engaging endless-running experience for players of all skill levels.

**Run fast. Collect magic. Survive the forest.**
